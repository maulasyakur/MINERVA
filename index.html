        MINERVA · Microgravity fire research, made explorable

        In space, fire has no up.

        Without gravity, a flame loses its direction but not its danger. MINERVA turns NASA's combustion experiments into answers for the habitats we plan to live in.

        Build a scenarioSee latest results
        Round, not pointed

        With no buoyancy to lift hot gas, a flame in orbit settles into a dim blue sphere instead of a teardrop.

        Airflow is oxygen

        In a spacecraft, the cabin fans become the flame's main oxygen supply. Switch them off and many flames starve.

        Earth tests can miss it

        Some materials have kept burning in microgravity below the oxygen level at which they go out on Earth.

        PROTOTYPE MINERVA is a research prototype. Spread rates are order-of-magnitude approximations from scaling laws or your indexed data, flame visuals are illustrative, and nothing here replaces certified material flammability testing.

        Built for the NASA Space Apps challenge to summarize, rank and interpret microgravity combustion findings for human space exploration. Background reading: NASA Glenn combustion science.
