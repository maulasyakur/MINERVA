              Flight path

              From scenario to safety readout in five stages

              1. 01

                Select a scenario

                Habitat, fuel, oxygen, pressure, ventilation and flow direction.

              2. 02

                Match test runs

                Nearest-neighbour search over the NASA runs, each variable scaled by its spread in the data.

              3. 03

                Summarise & approximate

                Report what the closest runs recorded, plus a labelled physics estimate of spread rate.

              4. 04

                Explain

                Rule-based notes tagged DATA or PHYSICS; on claude.ai, an AI write-up from the same evidence.

              5. 05

                Safety readout

                Hazard indicator, confidence from data density, and the supporting runs with their sources.

              Provenance

              What's in the data

              Every figure about the experiments is computed from data/psi_data.js, which scripts/build_data.py builds from the merged NASA PSI table. Each study in the results names its source file and row.

              PROTOTYPE MINERVA is a research prototype. Outcomes come from recorded NASA test runs, all done in microgravity, so Moon, Mars and Earth readouts are extrapolations. Spread rates are order-of-magnitude physics approximations, flame visuals are illustrative, and nothing here replaces certified material flammability testing.

              Built for the NASA Space Apps challenge. Data: NASA Physical Science Informatics (PSI) experiment tables. Background reading: NASA Glenn combustion science.
